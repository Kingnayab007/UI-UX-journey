01 / Introduction

MSM Entertainment ("MSM Entertainment", "we", "our" or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to handling personal information responsibly.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used and handled when you visit our website, submit a creator application, send a brand enquiry, or otherwise communicate with MSM Entertainment.

02 / Information We Collect

When you voluntarily submit information through our website, we may collect information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • City or location information you provide
  • Instagram username or profile
  • Follower count and creator category
  • Portfolio or other profile links
  • Company or brand name
  • Campaign type and estimated budget
  • Campaign briefs and enquiry details
  • Any other information you voluntarily provide to us

03 / How We Use Information

Information submitted to MSM Entertainment may be used to:

  • Review creator applications
  • Evaluate potential collaborations and partnerships
  • Respond to brand and campaign enquiries
  • Contact creators, brands or representatives regarding relevant opportunities
  • Manage our creator network and business relationships
  • Operate and improve our website and services
  • Prevent misuse, fraud or security-related activity
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations where required

Submitting a creator application does not guarantee acceptance into the MSM Entertainment creator network or participation in any campaign.

04 / Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be shared where reasonably necessary with service providers, technology providers, professional advisers, or relevant business partners involved in operating our services or evaluating a potential collaboration.

We may also disclose information when required by applicable law, regulation, legal process, or a valid request from an authorised authority.

05 / Data Storage & Security

We take reasonable administrative and technical measures intended to protect information submitted through our website from unauthorised access, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including managing enquiries, applications, business records and applicable legal requirements.

06 / Cookies & Technology

Our website and the infrastructure used to operate it may use cookies or similar technologies necessary for functionality, security, performance and session management.

Certain third-party services used to host or operate the website may also process limited technical information such as IP address, browser information, device information or request logs.

07 / Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or platforms, including social media profiles.

MSM Entertainment is not responsible for the privacy practices, content or security of third-party services. Their own privacy policies and terms apply when you visit those services.

08 / Your Information

If you have submitted personal information to MSM Entertainment and would like to request access, correction or deletion of that information, you may contact us.

We may need to verify your identity before processing certain requests. Some information may also need to be retained where required for legitimate business, security or legal purposes.

09 / Children's Privacy

Our website and services are not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate authorisation where such authorisation is required by applicable law.

10 / Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our website, services, business practices or applicable requirements.

The latest version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
